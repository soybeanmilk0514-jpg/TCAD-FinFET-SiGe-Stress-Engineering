## Terms

FR:
Source/Drain fin recess depth.

SlFin:
ChFin volume-averaged stress in the channel-length direction.

STE:
Ge-based nominal stress-normalized channel stress metric used by the team.

Ioff_norm / IdSat_norm:
Normalized electrical metrics exported with a common team extraction convention.
