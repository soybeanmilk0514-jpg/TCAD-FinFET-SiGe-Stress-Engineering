# References Used in the Final Team Report

The final report used the following literature as the main technical background.

1. M. Choi, V. Moroz, L. Smith, O. Penzin, “14 nm FinFET Stress Engineering with Epitaxial SiGe Source/Drain,” 2012 6th International Silicon-Germanium Technology and Device Meeting (ISTDM). DOI: 10.1109/ISTDM.2012.6222469

   - Background for epitaxial SiGe S/D stress engineering in FinFETs.

2. C. Qin, H. Yin, G. Wang, et al., “Study of sigma-shaped source/drain recesses for embedded-SiGe pMOSFETs,” Microelectronic Engineering, Vol. 181, pp. 22–28, 2017. DOI: 10.1016/j.mee.2017.07.001

   - Background for the influence of S/D recess geometry and depth on channel stress and device characteristics.

3. G. Eneman, P. Verheyen, R. Rooyackers, et al., “Scalability of the Si1−xGex Source/Drain Technology for the 45-nm Technology Node and Beyond,” IEEE Transactions on Electron Devices, Vol. 53, No. 7, pp. 1647–1656, 2006. DOI: 10.1109/TED.2006.876390

   - Background for scalability and design limits of SiGe S/D stress engineering.

4. P. M. Mooney, “Strain relaxation and dislocations in SiGe/Si structures,” Materials Science and Engineering R: Reports, Vol. 17, No. 3, pp. 105–146, 1996. DOI: 10.1016/S0927-796X(96)00192-1

   - Used to discuss the limitation that real high-Ge SiGe can exhibit relaxation and misfit defects not captured by the ideal coherent TCAD structure.

## Verification Note

During topic selection, the team found several cases where search summaries mixed device types, mechanisms, dates, or DOI information. The final report therefore retained only references that had been checked against the original source.
